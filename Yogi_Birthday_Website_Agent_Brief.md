## 1. THE MOST IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION

### The central idea

**“For Our Yogi — The Person Who Made Us Feel Like a Team.”**

Alternate short title: **“A Little World Called Yogi.”**

The emotional idea behind the entire experience is simple: people join teams, groups, projects and college circles, but some people make those spaces feel warmer and more human. Yogi is one of those people.

This is a birthday celebration from friends. The website should communicate:

- We are genuinely grateful that you are part of our lives and our team.
- You are energetic, cheerful, adorable, funny, caring and grounded.
- You work incredibly hard, contribute in multiple ways and still underestimate how much you do.
- You know how to keep your head when a situation gets chaotic.
- You made Ajinkya feel included even though he joined a group whose members had already known one another since Sem 1.
- You are someone with whom the group can talk for hours, whether the topic is serious, silly or completely random.
- You are an irreplaceable part of this group. We are celebrating you, not ranking you above the other friends.

### Tone of voice

Write like real college friends who are deeply fond of each other. Do not write like a company, a marketing agency, a wedding invitation, or an AI-generated greeting-card generator.

Use natural, conversational English, with small touches of the language and expressions already present in our conversations. Expressions such as these are welcome when they fit naturally:

- “Yogi”
- “BROHH”
- “arey” / “aree”
- “paraya”
- “touchwood”
- “nazar na lage”
- “Sister, please!”
- “how are you even like this?”
- “we could talk to you forever”

Do not force Hinglish or Marathi into every sentence. A few authentic phrases are much better than a page full of artificial slang.

### What this is NOT

Do not use romantic-couple language or concepts. Specifically remove or rewrite every reference to:

- girlfriend, partner, soulmate, lover, babe, baby, sweetheart, or couple
- “relationship start date” or “days together” counters
- long-distance relationship, distance between us, reunion, next meetup countdown
- “when we meet again” as a romantic reunion
- romantic declarations such as “I am in love with you” or “you are the love of my life”
- the old sample recipient/sender names and pet names in the starter project

Platonic affection is absolutely welcome. Saying “we love you”, “we are lucky to have you”, or “you are our favourite human” is appropriate in a close-friends context. Keep the emotional tone sincere rather than romantic.

### How Ajinkya first met Yogi

Ajinkya first met Yogi at **Umiya**, during an SIH-related meeting. It was a very natural, happy and comfortable first meeting. At that time, Yogi was already part of the SIH group with the others. - She is energetic, cheerful, cute, expressive and naturally happy to be around.

- She is someone the group can talk to for a very long time without running out of things to say.
- She is caring and thoughtful.
- She handles difficult situations and stressful moments with grace.
- She can help keep things calm when everything around the team becomes chaotic.

The visual experience should feel like a lovingly edited mini-film / memory scrapbook, not like a generic SaaS UI and not like a wedding page.

### Recommended direction

- Cinematic editorial layout with strong, beautiful image moments.
- Warm golden-hour / tungsten-light atmosphere that complements the uploaded restaurant and team photographs.
- A refined palette: warm cream, muted amber/gold, deep cocoa or charcoal, and one restrained secondary accent such as dusty rose or muted olive.
- Soft film grain or paper texture can be used sparingly.
- Elegant typography: a distinctive display serif or expressive editorial font for major headings, paired with a clean, highly readable sans-serif for body copy.
- Use generous spacing and thoughtful composition. The photos and words should be the main decoration.
- Make the page feel personal with small scrapbook elements, photo captions, handwritten-style accents, subtle framing and tasteful motion.
- Use confetti sparingly, only at meaningful reveal moments.
- Keep hearts subtle or avoid excessive heart decoration. This is a friendship/team tribute, not a romantic page.

### Avoid

- Neon AI gradients and generic purple-blue SaaS aesthetics.
- Excessive pink hearts, wedding motifs, rose petals or romantic visual shorthand.
- Too many competing colours, glows or animations.
- Large amounts of floating decoration that obscure the photos.
- Overly cute cartoon illustrations that make the page look childish.
- Text-heavy blocks that are difficult to read on a phone.

### Motion and cinematic moments

- Give the opening a clear sense of anticipation.
- Use a smooth reveal into the main story.
- Use scroll-based reveals sparingly and consistently.
- Create one or two memorable transitions between key sections.
- Keep photo lightbox navigation smooth and usable.
- The final group image and final birthday line should feel like the emotional ending of a short film.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid animation that causes motion sickness.
